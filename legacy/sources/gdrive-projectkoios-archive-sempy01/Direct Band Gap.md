#### Direct Semiconductor
Gallium arsenide ($\text{GaAs}$) is called a _direct semiconductor_ because it does not require a change in momentum for an electron transition from the valence band to the conduction band. 
#### Indirector Semiconductor
Silicon ($\text{Si}$) is called an _indirect semiconductor_ because a change of momentum is required in a transition. This difference between direct and indirect band structures is very important for light-emitting diodes and semiconductor lasers.
