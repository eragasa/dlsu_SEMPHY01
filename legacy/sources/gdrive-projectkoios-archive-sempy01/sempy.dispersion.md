
Parabolic relationship between energy and momentum of a free electron is provided in Equation 3.28, which we know as the [[_solidstate.dispersion curve]]
$$
\tag{Neaman 3.28}
  E=\frac{p^2}{2m}=\frac{\hbar^2 k^2}{2m}
$$
The $E$ vs $k$ curve near the the bottom of the conduction band can be approximated as a parabola.  This is also true for the top of the valence band.  In literature, these are known as electronic band diagrams.
