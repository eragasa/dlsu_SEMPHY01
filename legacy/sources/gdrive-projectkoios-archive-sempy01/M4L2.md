**degenerate semiconductor**.   A semiconductor when all donor atoms whose electron concentration or hole concentration is greater than the effective density of states, so that the Fermi energy is in the conduction band ($n$-type) or in the valence band ($p$-type).
## Glossary
**Acceptor atoms.** Impurity atoms added to a semiconductor to create a $p$-type material.
**Charge carrier**.  The electron and/or hole that moves inside the semiconductor and gives to electrical currents.
