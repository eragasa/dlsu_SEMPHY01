## 3) DOS-Based Carrier Densities (Sec. 4.1.2)

**Goal:** Evaluate \(n(E_F,T)\), \(p(E_F,T)\) from band-edge DOS.

**Use**
- Parabolic-band DOS near edges and FD occupation:
$$
  n = \int_{E_c}^{\infty} D_c(E) f(E;E_F,T)\,dE,\qquad
  p = \int_{-\infty}^{E_v} D_v(E)\,[1-f(E;E_F,T)]\,dE.
$$
- Nondegenerate approximation:
$$
  n \approx N_c\,e^{-(E_c-E_F)/k_BT},\quad
  p \approx N_v\,e^{-(E_F-E_v)/k_BT}.
$$

**Compute/plot**
- \(n(E_F)\) and \(p(E_F)\) at \(T=300\,\text{K}\) across \(E_F\in[E_v-0.2,\,E_c+0.2]\) eV.
- Overlay exact (numerical integral) vs. nondegenerate approximation.

**Deliverable:** Two plots with error bands or ratio “exact/approx”.

## 4) Fermi Level from Charge Neutrality (Secs. 4.3, 4.5, 4.6)

**Goal:** Solve for $E_F$ at given doping and temperature.

**Neutrality equation (no compensation):**
$$
n(E_F,T)+N_A^-(E_F,T)=p(E_F,T)+N_D^+(E_F,T).
$$

**Ionization models (shallow levels):**
$$
N_D^+=\frac{N_D}{1+g_D\,e^{(E_F-E_D)/k_BT}},\quad
N_A^-=\frac{N_A}{1+g_A\,e^{(E_A-E_F)/k_BT}}.
$$

**Tasks**
- Implement a root-finder for \(E_F\) given \((N_D,N_A,T)\).
- **Plots:**
  - \(E_F\) vs. \(N_D\) (n-type) at \(T=\{200,300,500\}\,\text{K}\).
  - \(E_F\) vs. \(T\) for \(N_D=10^{16}\,\text{cm}^{-3}\).
- Indicate freeze-out, extrinsic, and intrinsic regimes on the plots.

**Deliverable:** Two figures + short paragraph identifying regime boundaries.

## 5) Degenerate vs. Nondegenerate (Sec. 4.3.4)

**Goal:** Quantify when MB fails.

**Use**
- Reduced Fermi level $\eta=(E_F-E_c)/k_BT$.
- Fermi–Dirac integral \(F_{1/2}(\eta)\):
  \[
  n = N_c\,F_{1/2}(\eta).
  \]

**Compute/plot**
- \(F_{1/2}(\eta)\) numerically for \(\eta\in[-6,6]\).
- Compare \(n/N_c\) using \(F_{1/2}(\eta)\) vs. MB \(e^{\eta}\).

**Deliverable:** Plot of \(F_{1/2}(\eta)\) and relative error of MB.
