## 1. Band Structures
[[SzeLee3Ed.pdf#page=15|Sze Chapter 1]]
- E–k diagrams for metals, semiconductors, insulators.

##### Energy of a free electron
$$
	E=\frac{p^2}{2 m_0}
$$
##### Effective mass
$$
	E = \frac{p^2}{2 m_n}
$$
$$
	m_n \equiv 
	\left(
		\frac{\mathrm{d}^2E}
		     {\mathrm{d}\, p^2}
	\right)
$$
- Effective mass, density of states in 1D/2D/3D.
- Direct vs indirect gaps.
- Doping effects on Fermi level position.
## 2. Semiconductor in Equilibrium
- Carrier concentrations from Fermi–Dirac statistics.
- Intrinsic, n-type, and p-type semiconductors.
- Charge neutrality and mass action law.
- Temperature dependence of carrier density.
## 3. Carrier Transport Phenomena
- Drift and mobility, scattering mechanisms.
- Diffusion, Einstein relation.
- Conductivity and resistivity tensor.
- Hall effect.
## 4. Nonequilibrium Excess Carriers in Semiconductors
- Optical and electrical generation.
- Recombination mechanisms: radiative, SRH, Auger.
- Continuity equation.
- Carrier lifetime and diffusion length.
## 5. pn Junction
[[SzeLee3Ed.pdf|Sze 2012, Chapter 3]]
- Built-in potential and depletion region.
- Charge distribution and Poisson’s equation.
- Energy band diagrams under equilibrium.
## 6. pn Junction Diode
- Current–voltage characteristics (ideal and non-ideal).
- Minority carrier injection and diffusion.
- Reverse breakdown mechanisms.
## 7. Metal–Semiconductor and Semiconductor Heterojunctions
- Schottky barrier formation.
- Ohmic contacts.
- Heterojunction band alignment (Type I, II, III).
## 8. Bipolar Transistors
- Structure and operation (active, saturation, cutoff modes).
- Ebers–Moll model.
- Current gain, base transport factor.
## 9. MOSFET
- MOS capacitor electrostatics.
- Threshold voltage derivation.
- I–V characteristics in linear and saturation regions.
- Short-channel effects (introductory).
