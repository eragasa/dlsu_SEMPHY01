# LA2 source notes

For every transport, recombination, dielectric, junction, contact, and
high-field parameter, record:

- full citation or stable URL;
- exact material phase or polytype;
- sample preparation and compensation when reported;
- temperature and concentration range;
- crystallographic direction;
- definition and measurement method;
- unit conversion;
- whether the value is measured, fitted, or calculated;
- uncertainty or spread across sources; and
- where the parameter enters the LA2 model.

Separate calibration sources from validation sources.
